## Priority Issues

### [P1] Light-theme accent heading contrast

Location: `app/globals.css`, lines 4 and 58. `--accent` is used as heading text on the light paper background, with an estimated contrast near 1.09:1. Keep heading text ink-colored and use lime as a marker, underline or adjacent block. Suggested command: `$impeccable colorize app/globals.css`.

### [P1] Consulting-style intake before proof

Location: `app/page.tsx`, lines 117, 135 and 183. URL, product and audience are required before the user sees concrete product evidence, while the adjacent image is generic. Add a real redacted Finding/report excerpt and explain public-page scope, three free findings and approximate timing. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify app/page.tsx`.

### [P2] Framework and Method overlap

Location: `app/page.tsx`, lines 208 and 224. The five framework dimensions and three method cards answer similar questions without a visible mapping. Consolidate or show five dimensions producing three free findings. Suggested command: `$impeccable distill app/page.tsx`.

### [P2] “signups” is narrower than the product

The product also serves demo requests, leads and purchases. Use “conversions” or “signups, leads, or sales” unless signup-only analysis is intentional. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify app/page.tsx`.

### [P2/P3] Paid value and data boundary are hidden

Pricing and the public-page-only boundary are too far from the form. Add a concise factual line near the form explaining the free review and optional $29 report. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify app/page.tsx`.

## Persona Red Flags

- Busy Indie Hacker: three required fields feel like consulting intake for a quick URL review.
- B2B SaaS or ecommerce founder: “signups” can imply the product is not for demos, leads or purchases.
- Designer/marketer: no report preview or shareable evidence artifact beyond one Stripe Teardown.
