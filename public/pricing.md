# SiteLens pricing

SiteLens helps founders identify the first website change worth fixing. Pricing below is for the current SiteLens Phase 0 product.

## Free website review

- Price: $0
- Includes: a page score and three page-specific findings.
- Analysis: homepage positioning, clarity, trust signals, conversion path, and page copy.
- Input: a public website URL, one-sentence product description, and target audience.
- Limitation: the free review is qualitative and does not estimate a conversion rate.

## Deep Growth Report

- Price: $29 USD, one time
- Includes: prioritized diagnosis, homepage structure recommendation, hero rewrite direction, CTA rewrite direction, and a three-week action plan.
- Delivery: the paid report is unlocked on the original SiteLens report page after payment confirmation.
- Limitation: SiteLens does not access private analytics, run experiments, or guarantee conversion, search ranking, or revenue results.

## Payment

Payment is handled by Waffo Pancake through the SiteLens checkout flow. No subscription is required for the Deep Growth Report.
