# SiteLens free access

SiteLens helps founders identify the first website change worth fixing. During the public beta, every review is free.

## Public-beta website review

- Price: $0
- Includes: a page score and three page-specific findings.
- Analysis: homepage positioning, clarity, trust signals, conversion path, page copy, metadata, accessibility, and public security signals.
- Input: a public website URL, one-sentence product description, and target audience.
- Method: deterministic rules applied to observable page evidence.
- Access: no account, email, subscription, or payment is required.
- Limitation: the review is qualitative and does not estimate a conversion rate or guarantee a business outcome.
